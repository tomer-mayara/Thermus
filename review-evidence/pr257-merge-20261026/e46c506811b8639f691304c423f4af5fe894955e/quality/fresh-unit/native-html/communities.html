Arrows point to what a community depends on, thicker for more references. A red dashed arrow is the one to cut to break a cycle.

How to read this
  • A box is a community: packages that depend on each other more than on the rest, read from the dependencies alone, never from the folder names
  • A depends on B
  • Thicker: more references
  • What nobody depends on sits at the top, what everything rests on at the bottom
  • Red dashed, climbing back up: it closes a cycle, and it is the lightest to cut
  • Dashed grey box: the shared kernel, used by everyone, part of no community
  • Under a line: linked to no other community
