# FS-03: private policy bundle and signup operations

Started October 16, 2026, after FS-02 merged in PR 123.

## Scope

Prepare complete private terms, a privacy notice, and exact agreement wording.
Retain their byte copies, versions, and SHA-256 hashes outside Git. Update the
manual onboarding, signup recovery, revocation, and deletion procedures to
match FS-01 and FS-02. Prepare a separate existing-user transition proposal.

Signup stays off. This ticket publishes no policy, supplies no production
document bundle, sends no notice, and changes no provider record or user access.
Approval of operating defaults does not approve complete policy text. Keep
document approval, publication, transition execution, and signup opening as
separate decisions.

## Acceptance specification

These are document and operator-procedure scenarios. Evaluate them by a
tabletop review; they do not require new software or provider writes.

1. **Given** the approved operating defaults and implemented signup flow,
   **when** the reviewer opens the private bundle, **then** it contains complete
   terms, notice, and agreement text, distinct versions, exact byte hashes,
   approval status, and identified factual gaps. A later edit uses a new version.
2. **Given** an uncertain activation, a repeated request, or a removed membership,
   **when** the operator follows recovery instructions, **then** reads determine
   the observed state. No automatic grant replay, role repair, record replacement,
   or unrelated access change is authorized.
3. **Given** an access-removal or whole-account deletion request, **when** the
   operator follows the runbook, **then** it distinguishes access removal from
   deletion of the retained signup record, controls in-flight activation, records
   scope and approval, and explains retained evidence and provider limits.
4. **Given** existing users with different access and agreement evidence,
   **when** the owner reviews the transition proposal, **then** the proposal
   identifies the inventory, notices, cutoff decision, evidence handling, and
   separate execution authority. No prior sign-in becomes agreement evidence.
5. **Given** the completed package, **when** the named operator checks readiness,
   **then** ownership and the restricted record store are explicit. Missing
   approvals or backup readiness remain visible release prerequisites.

Initial review: scenarios were not met. The private drafts were editorial
instructions rather than complete documents; the runbooks omitted self-serve
acceptance and retained signup records. Operator and store assignment were pending.

## Release boundaries

The manifest status is `review-only-unapproved`; it has no effective date. Later
text or date changes require new versions and hashes. Preserve this review copy
and record any complete-text approval against the exact manifest separately.
Do not reuse a version for changed bytes or infer agreement from a provider
marker without checking the matching retained text and evidence.

FS-04 must connect the approved bundle to production composition as well as
prepare matching marketing and publication artifacts. That handoff was implicit
in the original plan and is now explicit: the current production loader supplies
no documents, so enabling signup now fails startup. This ticket deliberately
adds no runtime bundle. Verify readable agreement presentation and the matching
approved text before publication. Keep signup off throughout that preparation.

Publication, existing-user transition execution, and signup opening remain
separate approvals. FS-05 checks deployment and opening. Do not present unknown
provider purge periods as a seven-day or thirty-day erasure promise.
